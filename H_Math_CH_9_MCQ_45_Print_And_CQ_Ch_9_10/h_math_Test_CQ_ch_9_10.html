১. $A = 2\log_k x - \log_k (3 + x)$ এবং $B = \frac{u-1}{\log_p (qr)} = \frac{v-1}{\log_q (rp)} = \frac{w-1}{\log_r (pq)}$
ক.$F(x) = \log\left(\frac{8}{x+5}\right)$ হলে, $F$ এর ডোমেন নির্ণয় কর।২
খ.$A = 0$ হলে দেখাও যে, $x = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \sqrt{13})$৪

২. $\frac{\log_k x}{p^2+pq+q^2} = \frac{\log_k y}{q^2+qr+r^2} = \frac{\log_k z}{r^2+rp+p^2}$ এবং $Q = m^{4b}.n^{8+2b} - m^{7b}.n^{5-b}$
ক.$\log_{10}(a^2 - 11a + 40) = 1$ হলে, $a$ এর মান নির্ণয় কর।২
খ.দেখাও যে, $x^{p-q}.y^{q-r}.z^{r-p} = 1$.৪

ক.প্রমাণ কর যে, $0! = 1$২

1. $A = 2\log_k x - \log_k (3 + x)$ and $B = \frac{u-1}{\log_p (qr)} = \frac{v-1}{\log_q (rp)} = \frac{w-1}{\log_r (pq)}$[cite: 1]
a.If $F(x) = \log\left(\frac{8}{x+5}\right)$, determine the domain of $F$.[cite: 1]2
b.If $A = 0$, show that $x = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \sqrt{13})$[cite: 1]4
c.If $B = 1$, prove that $uv + vw + uw - uvw = 0$[cite: 1]4
2. $\frac{\log_k x}{p^2+pq+q^2} = \frac{\log_k y}{q^2+qr+r^2} = \frac{\log_k z}{r^2+rp+p^2}$ and $Q = m^{4b}.n^{8+2b} - m^{7b}.n^{5-b}$[cite: 2]
a.If $\log_{10}(a^2 - 11a + 40) = 1$, find the value of $a$.[cite: 2]2
b.Show that $x^{p-q}.y^{q-r}.z^{r-p} = 1$.[cite: 2]4
c.If $Q = 0$, prove that $b\log_k\left(\frac{m}{n}\right) = \log_k n$.[cite: 2]4
3. $A = \left(y - \frac{x}{2}\right)^6$ and $B = r - 96x + sx^2 + \dots$[cite: 3]
a.Prove that $0! = 1$[cite: 3]2
b.If $A = B$, find the values of $y, s,$ and $r$.[cite: 3]4
c.If $y = 2$ in expression $A$, expand up to $x^3$ in ascending powers of $x$ and using this find the approximate value of $(1.05)^6$ up to four decimal places.[cite: 3]4
4. $\left(2x^2 - \frac{1}{2x^3}\right)^{10}$ and $\left(x^2 + \frac{y}{x}\right)^6$ are two binomial expressions.[cite: 4]
a.Find the middle term of the first binomial expression.[cite: 4]2
b.Find the term independent of $x$ in the expansion of the first binomial and find its value.[cite: 4]4
c.If the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion of the second binomial expression is 540, find the value of $y$.[cite: 4]4
